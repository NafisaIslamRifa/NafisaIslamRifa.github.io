## SSC and HSC Scholarships

Awarded merit-based scholarships for exceptional academic performance in **Secondary School Certificate (SSC)** and **Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC)** education board examinations in Bangladesh.

### Key Highlights:
- **Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Scholarship (2017):** Secured **191st rank among 152,783 candidates** in the national education board examination and received a merit scholarship.
- **Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Scholarship (2019):** Secured **473rd rank among 123,616 candidates** in the national education board examination and received a merit scholarship.
- Recognized for consistent academic excellence at the secondary and higher secondary education levels.
