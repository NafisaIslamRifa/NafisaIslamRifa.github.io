I am pleased to have served as a **Reviewer** for the **Women in Machine Learning (WiML) Workshop** at **NeurIPS 2026**.

**Workshop website:** [Women in Machine Learning Workshop at NeurIPS 2026](https://sites.google.com/wimlworkshop.org/wimlworkshopneurips2026/)

As a reviewer, I evaluated submitted research papers covering topics related to **Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and related areas**. My responsibilities included assessing the **novelty, technical quality, methodological soundness, experimental design, and clarity** of each submission, while providing constructive feedback to help authors strengthen their work.
