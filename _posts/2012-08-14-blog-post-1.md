I am pleased to serve as a **Reviewer** for the **MI4MedFM Workshop** at **MICCAI 2026**.
**Workshop website:** [MI4MedFM 2026](https://mi4medfm.github.io/2026/)

As a reviewer, I evaluate submitted research papers on topics related to **Medical Foundation Models**, **Medical Image Analysis**, **Computer Vision**, and **Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare**. My responsibilities include assessing the novelty, technical quality, experimental design, clarity of each submission while providing constructive feedback to help authors improve their work.
