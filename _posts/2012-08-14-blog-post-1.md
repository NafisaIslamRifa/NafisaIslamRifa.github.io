I am pleased to serve as a **Reviewer** for the **MI4MedFM Workshop** at **MICCAI 2026**.
**Workshop website:** [MI4MedFM 2026](https://mi4medfm.github.io/)

As a reviewer, I evaluate submitted research papers on topics related to **Medical Foundation Models**, **Medical Image Analysis**, **Computer Vision**, and **Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare**. My responsibilities include assessing the novelty, technical quality, experimental design, clarity, and potential impact of each submission while providing constructive feedback to help authors improve their work.

Serving as a reviewer is a valuable opportunity to contribute to the research community, stay informed about the latest developments in medical AI, and support the dissemination of high-quality scientific research.

Thank you to the MI4MedFM organizing committee for the opportunity to contribute to the peer-review process.
