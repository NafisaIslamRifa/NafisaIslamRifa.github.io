## Hi, I'm Nafisa Islam Rifa 👋

I am an **MSc Data Science** student at the **University of Greenwich, UK**, and a **B.Sc. graduate in Computer Science and Engineering** from **United International University (UIU), Bangladesh** .

My undergraduate research was supervised by **[Dr. Muhammad Nomani Kabir](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=J0V0RYkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)**, and I am currently pursuing my MSc dissertation under the supervision of **[Dr. Peter Soar](https://www.gre.ac.uk/people/rep/faculty-of-engineering-and-science/peter-soar)**.

My research interests include **Computer Vision**, **Medical Image Analysis**, **Vision-Language Models**, **Explainable AI (XAI)**, **Large Language Models**, and **Video Understanding**.

I am passionate about developing trustworthy AI systems that solve real-world problems, particularly in healthcare, through explainable and multimodal artificial intelligence.
