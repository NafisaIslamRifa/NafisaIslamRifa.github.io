I hold an MSc in Data Science with Distinction from the University of Greenwich, UK, and a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from United International University (UIU), Bangladesh.

My undergraduate research was supervised by [Dr. Muhammad Nomani Kabir](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=J0V0RYkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao), and my MSc dissertation was supervised by [Dr. Peter Soar](https://www.gre.ac.uk/people/rep/faculty-of-engineering-and-science/peter-soar).

My research interests include Computer Vision, Medical Image Analysis, Vision-Language Models, Explainable AI (XAI), Large Language Models, and Video Understanding.

I am passionate about developing trustworthy AI systems that solve real-world problems, particularly in healthcare, through explainable and multimodal artificial intelligence. Alongside my research, I also build applied generative AI systems — including agentic, retrieval-augmented pipelines — to explore how these methods translate from research settings into practical, real-world tools.

I am currently seeking research and industry roles in machine learning, medical imaging and applied AI in the UK.
